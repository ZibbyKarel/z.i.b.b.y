Communications owns triage of inbound conversation and every outbound word: gated
replies, comms style per project, and the notify-only email posture —
graduating toward more autonomy only with evidence. It is the hlas navenek
(voice to the outside world). Built as plumbing, not yet a voice with a
policy.
