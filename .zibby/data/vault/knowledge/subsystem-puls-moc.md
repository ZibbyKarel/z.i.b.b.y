Puls owns every inbound heartbeat: channel polling, calendar, and CI/CD
monitors — the monitor seam Sentry will join next. It is the tep systému
(system pulse) that keeps ZIBBY aware of the outside world on a schedule, not
just when addressed. Mostly built, needs ownership.
